# BI-Portifolio
Power BI - Dashboards

Awesome Chocolates – Sales & Performance Overview

An end-to-end commercial performance dashboard tracking confectionery distribution, product margins, and regional sales trends across retail partners.

Total Revenue & Net Sales: Overall sales volume broken down by currency, store tier, and payment method.

Profitability Margins: Gross profit margin and operating margin percentages by product line (dark, milk, artisan truffles).

Product Category Performance: Top 10 bestselling items vs. low-velocity SKUs by volume and net margin.

Geographic Breakdown: Sales by territory, regional distribution center, and fulfillment route.

Growth Benchmarks: Month-over-Month (MoM) and Year-over-Year (YoY) revenue variance.

Christmas Seasonal Sales Analysis

A high-velocity retail analytics dashboard evaluating holiday shopping behavior, promotional efficacy, and inventory turnover during peak fourth-quarter volume.

Holiday Revenue Velocity: Daily and weekly run-rate tracking leading up to December 25th.

Promotional Lift & Discount Rate: Comparison of baseline sales against campaign spikes (Black Friday, Cyber Week, Flash Sales).

Sell-Through Rate (STR): Percentage of seasonal inventory sold versus remaining units at risk of post-holiday clearance.

Average Order Value (AOV): Basket size and item count per transaction compared to non-holiday quarters.

Return & Exchange Rate: Early post-holiday return volume and impact on net realized revenue.

Project Management & Delivery Portfolio

An operational tracking dashboard designed to monitor project health, milestone schedules, budget consumption, and team workload across active initiatives.

Schedule & Milestone Progress: Overall percentage complete, task status distribution (Completed, In Progress, At Risk, Blocked), and upcoming deadlines.

Budget Tracking: Total approved budget, actual spend, planned value, and projected cost to completion.

Earned Value Metrics: Cost Variance (CV) and Schedule Variance (SV) to identify cost overruns or timeline slippage early.

Resource Utilization: Hours logged per team member, capacity bandwidth, and bottleneck allocations.

Issue & Risk Exposure: Open action items categorized by severity level and average resolution turnaround time.
